1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & CORE MISSION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DevKo is an independent, high-performance financial policy discovery and eligibility
intelligence platform. It allows citizens, entrepreneurs, and MSME business owners
to search, evaluate, and navigate financial schemes, government subsidies, and
business grants.

CRITICAL IDENTITY & CONSTRAINTS:
- 100% INDEPENDENT: DevKo is NOT an assistant of the "myScheme" platform, nor is it
  bound to or affiliated with any government agency or ministry. It operates
  autonomously as a policy discovery engine.
- ZERO AI HALLUCINATIONS: Eligibility decisions are evaluated using deterministic,
  mathematical Boolean and AST (Abstract Syntax Tree) logic derived from official
  public circulars. Generative AI is NEVER used to guess or hallucinate statutory
  eligibility.
- ZERO EVALUATION / HACKATHON JARGON: All hackathon evaluator banners, tour bars,
  and judge-specific labels have been completely removed.
- MODERN FINTECH DESIGN: Designed with an ultra-modern aesthetic inspired by modern
  fintech apps (Stripe, Linear, Ramp) — faint green accents (#f0fdf4), crisp white
  surfaces, subtle glassmorphism (backdrop-blur), smooth lift transitions, and
  meticulous mobile responsiveness across all devices (360px to 4K).

6. DEVELOPER GUIDELINES FOR ANY AI WORKING ON THIS CODEBASE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Brand Integrity:
   - Always maintain the platform name as "DevKo" or "DevKo Schemes".
   - NEVER introduce claims of being an official government portal, an assistant of
     myScheme, or bound to any government department.
   - Keep the independent platform disclaimer in footers and readmes.
2. Aesthetics & Design:
   - Keep the clean, modern aesthetic: crisp white cards, faint emerald backgrounds
     (#f0fdf4), slate typography, subtle borders (border-slate-200/80), and modern
     badges.
   - Avoid dated/clunky government portal look or harsh bright colors.
   - Use .transition-lift on interactive cards and buttons.
3. Mobile Responsiveness:
   - Never use rigid widths like min-w-[280px] on parent cards without responsive
     modifiers (use w-full sm:w-auto sm:min-w-[280px]).
   - Always wrap wide tables in <div className="overflow-x-auto"> with
     <table className="w-full min-w-[640px]"> so cells don't collapse.
   - Use px-3 sm:px-6 for main containers to maximize mobile screen utilization.
4. Deterministic Logic:
   - Preserve the deterministic Boolean AST rule evaluation pattern in
     src/data/mockData.js and src/components/schemes/RuleRow.jsx.
5. Testing:
   - Always run `npm run build` to verify zero TypeScript/JSX/bundling regressions.
